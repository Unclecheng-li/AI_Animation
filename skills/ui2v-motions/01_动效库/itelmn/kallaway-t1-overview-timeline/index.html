What Happens After You Post
00 – 30 min
The Algorithm Samples Your Audience
YouTube shows the video to a small seed batch of subscribers to measure click-through and watch time before deciding how wide to push it.
1 – 6 hours
Velocity Determines Distribution
If early signals are strong, the video enters the browse feed and suggested column for non-subscribers — this window is the entire game.
24 – 72 hours
Long-Tail Search Traffic Kicks In
The video gets indexed for keyword queries. Evergreen content compounds here for months — but only if early watch time held above 40%.
