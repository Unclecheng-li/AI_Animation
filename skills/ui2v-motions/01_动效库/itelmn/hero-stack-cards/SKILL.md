# Hero Stack Cards

Three warm orange cards deal into a staggered stack beside a product title. Function: feature deck reveal, like briefing slides fanning open.

## Purpose
- Product storytelling with tactile cards
- Layered depth without 3D libraries
- Warm palette contrast against cool neon heroes

## Specs
- Cards enter with rotation settle
- Front card continues a gentle lift

## Usage
Preview locally then publish with ui2v motion publish.

## Notes
Keep three cards max; four starts to hide copy on lower layers.
