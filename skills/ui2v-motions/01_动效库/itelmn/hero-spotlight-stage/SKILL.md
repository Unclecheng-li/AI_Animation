# Hero Spotlight Stage

Red curtains part, a spotlight drops, and the feature title takes center stage. Function: theatrical product debut, not dashboard chrome.

## Purpose
- Feature launches and keynote moments
- Curtain wipe plus spotlight language
- Dramatic contrast after soft ambient pieces

## Specs
- Curtains translate outward
- Spotlight scales from the top and drifts

## Usage
Publish with ui2v, then consider as a carousel opener.

## Notes
Keep actor text centered; off-center titles fight the spotlight ellipse.
