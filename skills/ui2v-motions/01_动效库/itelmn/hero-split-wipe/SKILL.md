# Hero Split Wipe

A magazine-style split screen: charcoal left, cream right, joined by a crimson wipe bar. Functionally this is a contrast reveal for editorial storytelling, not a glow loop.

## Purpose
- Editorial and campaign heroes
- Hard graphic wipes instead of soft fades
- Demonstrating opposing panel choreography

## Specs
- 1920x1080, 4.5s, Georgia display type
- Left/right panels slide in after a vertical wipe

## Usage
Install with ui2v, preview in HyperFrames, then pin if needed.

## Notes
Keep the wipe thin. A thick divider reads as a third panel and weakens the binary contrast.
