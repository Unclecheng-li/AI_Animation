# Hero Badge Earn

A gold medal pops in with red ribbons and a dashed celebration ring. Function: **achievement unlock** for gamified or milestone moments.

## Purpose

- Rewards, onboarding completion, ship celebrations
- Back.out medal impact
- Warm gold against dark slate

## Specs

- Medal scales past 1.0 then settles
- Ribbons drop after the medal lands
- Entry: `index.html`

## Usage

```bash
npx hyperframes preview
ui2v motion publish . --version 1.0.0
```

## Notes

Keep medal copy to two short words. Longer phrases wrap awkwardly inside the circle.
