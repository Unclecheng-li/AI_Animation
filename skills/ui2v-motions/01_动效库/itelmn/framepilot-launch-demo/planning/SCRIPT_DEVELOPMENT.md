# Script development — CutSteward 12-second launch demo

Audience: international English-speaking creators, studios, and AI-agent builders.  
Format: concise English on-screen copy over original project-authored music; no narration.
Rule: never claim a provider action, approval, output, or customer result that the demo does not visibly prove.

## Pass 1 — complete idea

Turn one clear outcome into an entire AI-video production. CutSteward coordinates research, generation, editing, and review while keeping the work visible and every important decision under your control.

Verdict: truthful, but too broad and too long for twelve seconds.

## Pass 2 — remove generic claims

One brief becomes a production plan. Research, generation, editing, and review stay visible. Every important action is recorded, and the final decision stays with you.

Verdict: stronger, but “every action is recorded” overstates the current evidence model.

## Pass 3 — align the copy to visible proof

AI video moves fast. Start with the outcome. One accountable workflow keeps research, generation, editing, and verification under your control. The work stays visible. Exact approvals and real receipts support trusted output.

Verdict: evidence-aligned, but still too sentence-led for a fast visual film.

## Pass 4 — locked visual copy

1. `AI VIDEO MOVES FAST.` / `START WITH THE OUTCOME`
2. `ONE ACCOUNTABLE WORKFLOW.` / `RESEARCH · GENERATE · EDIT · VERIFY` / `UNDER YOUR CONTROL.`
3. `THE WORK STAYS VISIBLE.` / `PLAN LOCKED · SOURCE ROUTED · OUTPUT VERIFIED` / `NO BLIND SIDE EFFECTS.`
4. `EXACT APPROVALS.` / `REAL RECEIPTS.` / `AI VIDEO, UNDER YOUR CONTROL.` / `BUILD WITH CUTSTEWARD`

The phrases are distributed unchanged across `SCRIPT.md` and the storyboard.
The CTA remains fully resolved for at least 1.5 seconds while the original music
bed carries the close. No speech track is part of the public master.
