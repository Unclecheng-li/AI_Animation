# Hero Neon Pulse

A 4.5 second HyperFrames hero built around expanding neon rings and a luminous core. Designed for UI2V homepage carousels that need a live, signal-like energy without narration.

## Purpose

- Status and realtime product heroes
- Silent loops that read clearly at thumbnail scale
- Demonstrating seek-safe GSAP scale pulses

## Specs

- 1920x1080 @ 30fps, duration 4.5s
- Entry file: index.html
- Timeline: window.__timelines.main

## Usage

1. Install: npx ui2v@latest install hero-neon-pulse
2. Preview with HyperFrames Studio
3. Pin as a homepage hero slide when public

## Notes

Keep the core bright and the outer rings translucent so the title on the left rail stays readable against the dark field.
