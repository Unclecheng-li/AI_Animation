`upbeat-data-loop.mp3` is a normalized template master of “City Loop” by wipics.

- Source: https://opengameart.org/content/city-loop-0
- Original file: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/city-loop_0.mp3
- License: CC0 1.0 Universal / public domain
- Changes: reduced by 7 dB and encoded as a 44.1 kHz stereo MP3
- Template playback: HyperFrames owns playback and selects the composition’s declared span; the registered GSAP timeline applies transition-synchronized opening and closing fades
- Coverage: the bundled master covers quick, standard, and deliberate presets up to its 56-second source duration; longer compositions should replace it with a longer local track
