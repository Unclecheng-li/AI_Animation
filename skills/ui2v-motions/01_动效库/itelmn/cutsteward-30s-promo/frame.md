# CutSteward promo frame system

Dark graphite, warm ivory, and restrained brass. The live CutSteward UI remains ungraded and readable inside a dimensional browser frame; the surrounding composition supplies depth through a soft warm pool, one structural rule, and quiet foreground metadata. Never recolor or redraw product pixels.

## Typography roles

1. **Editorial hook:** 82–104px, heavy sans, upper-left; no pill.
2. **Documentary subtitle:** 34–42px, ivory with local shadow, lower-left around 70–78% frame height; maximum two lines.
3. **Step callout:** 20–24px mono label plus 42–54px sans, top or side corridor; never over the browser window.
4. **CTA:** 34px wordmark, 78–92px serif promise, 28–32px action label; brand → promise → action, then still.

## Product-frame contract

The browser frame occupies roughly 72–82% of canvas width and no more than 74% of canvas height. Keep at least 64px between captions/callouts and product chrome. Cursor/ripple may appear only inside the screen recording. One browser/device object at a time; no duplicate shell, no glow loop, no floating card stack.

## Motion contract

Hard or motivated match cuts dominate. Use one short opacity crossfade only into the CTA. Screen footage may receive a finite 2–3% push or 18–32px pan on an outer wrapper; source UI pixels stay untouched. Each beat builds, breathes, resolves, then becomes still. Effects must have one named narrative job.
