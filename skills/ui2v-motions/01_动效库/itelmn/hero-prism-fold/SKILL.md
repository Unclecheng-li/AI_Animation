# Hero Prism Fold

Three translucent color planes fold into depth beside the title. The motion is about layered cards, not particles.

## Purpose

- Brand and design-system heroes
- Showing rotateY-friendly transforms that stay seek-safe
- Color storytelling for UI2V

## Specs

- Duration 4.5s at 1920x1080
- Panels enter from the right with decreasing opacity

## Usage

Install, preview, and add to homepage management when ready.

## Notes

Avoid stacking more than three panels; additional layers muddy the silhouette at thumbnail size.
