# Hero Lockup Sting

A centered UI2V wordmark scales into place, a gradient rule draws underneath, and a HyperFrames Registry tagline fades up. Short brand sting for opens and outros.

## Purpose

- Brand intros on homepage and launch pages
- Minimal lockup demos with no external logo file
- Teaching letter-spacing animation safely

## Specs

- Centered composition, 4.5s, 1920x1080
- Mark, rule, and tag on three tracks

## Usage

Install and preview. Suitable as the first slide in a hero carousel.

## Notes

Keep the mark huge; shrinking it loses the sting impact at full bleed.
