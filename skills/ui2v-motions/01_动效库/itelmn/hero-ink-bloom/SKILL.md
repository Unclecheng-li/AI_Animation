# Hero Ink Bloom

Soft blurred blooms expand and drift like ink in water. The piece gives UI2V a warmer organic option next to geometric heroes.

## Purpose

- Lifestyle and creative-brand heroes
- Soft focus backgrounds behind sharp type
- Variety in the registry carousel

## Specs

- 4.5s, 1920x1080, mix-blend screen blooms
- Title remains left-aligned and high contrast

## Usage

Install with ui2v and preview. Blooms use fixed transforms only.

## Notes

Do not remove the blur; hard-edged circles break the ink metaphor.
