# Hero Horizon Scan

Grid lines fade in, a glowing horizon locks, and a vertical scan sweeps the frame. Ideal for sci-fi or infrastructure product heroes.

## Purpose

- Platform and infrastructure narratives
- Clean sweep transitions without video footage
- Demonstrating long-travel x tweens

## Specs

- Entry index.html, duration 4.5s
- Horizon plus scan on separate tracks

## Usage

Publish via ui2v motion publish after local lint passes.

## Notes

Keep the horizon near the lower third so title blocks above remain unobstructed.
