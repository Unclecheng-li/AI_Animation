# Hero Dial Gauge

An instrument cluster needle sweeps while a large percent counts up. Function: capacity or health readout rather than a marketing slogan.

## Purpose
- Ops, performance, and reliability heroes
- Analog gauge language in a digital product
- Pairing rotational motion with numeric onUpdate

## Specs
- Needle rotates from -120 to about 48 degrees
- Counter lands near 86 percent

## Usage
ui2v install hero-dial-gauge, scrub the sweep, then publish.

## Notes
Do not animate font metrics; only the numeric textContent updates.
