TDrive
A private, cross-platform drive powered by your own Telegram account.

Organize files in a desktop app, stream Telegram media, mount your drive in the
operating system, or manage it from the command line.

Store and organize
- Private My Drive and collaborative shared drives
- Nested folders, rename, move, drag and drop, search, and delete
- Files larger than 2 GB are split across Telegram messages and presented as one file

Import and transfer
- Upload files or folders
- Import zip, tar, and tar.gz archives with optional extraction
- Concurrent cancellable transfers with progress, speed, and retries

Preview and stream
- Drive-wide photo gallery
- Preview images, PDF, text, code, audio, and video
- Stream media directly from Telegram

Share and collaborate
- Shared drives backed by Telegram megagroups
- Invite links, approvals, and uploader attribution

Mount and automate
- Mount TDrive in Finder, Explorer, or a Linux file manager
- Use the CLI for scripts, imports, synchronization, vault access, and mounts

Protect and recover
- Optional client-side XChaCha20-Poly1305 encryption for personal-drive file contents
- Live synchronization
- Rebuild local SQLite state from Telegram history
