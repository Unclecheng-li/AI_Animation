# Dark Graph Paper Template

## Style Prompt

A full-screen dark-mode graph paper takeover for text overlays: near-black matte paper, faint measured grid lines, subtle cyan/green light, and low-contrast procedural texture that shifts like a scanner passing over technical paper. Motion should be ambient, precise, text-safe, and loop cleanly over an eight-second cycle.

## Colors

- Canvas black: `#030507`
- Paper blue-black: `#071015`
- Fine grid cyan: `rgba(103, 232, 249, 0.12)`
- Major grid green: `rgba(110, 231, 183, 0.14)`
- Soft edge light: `rgba(125, 211, 252, 0.10)`

## Typography

No required text in this prototype. If reused with copy, use `IBM Plex Sans` and `IBM Plex Mono` to stay compatible with the AI Opportunity overlay family.

## What Not To Do

- Do not change the existing tan crumpled-paper template.
- Do not make the background bright blue or purple.
- Do not use fast strobing, glitch jumps, or loud neon fills.
- Do not add foreground instructional text to the reusable background.
- Do not add constellation paths, nodes, labels, or shapes that compete with future text.
