## Rejection rules

- Do not say every AI agent works; say `compatible AI agents` only when needed.
- Do not imply the sample plan generated the separate certified conformance delivery.
- Do not claim cloud generation, provider connection, spend, upload, or publishing.
- Do not call `Journal live` an executing agent.
- If the real UI cannot prove a clause, remove the clause before TTS; never fake the proof.
