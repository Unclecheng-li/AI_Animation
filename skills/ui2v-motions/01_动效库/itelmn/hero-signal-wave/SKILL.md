# Hero Signal Wave

An equalizer field of twenty-four bars rises and settles under a bold title. Use it when the product story needs audible energy without actual audio.

## Purpose

- Creator tools and media product heroes
- Registry samples of staggered scaleY motion
- Homepage variety beyond orbital motifs

## Specs

- 1920x1080, 4.5s, GSAP paused timeline
- Entry index.html with wave track and copy track

## Usage

npx ui2v@latest install hero-signal-wave then preview. Bars are deterministic heights, never Math.random.

## Notes

Bar colors stay cool cyan-blue so they do not compete with pink accents used in other hero packs.
