## Style Prompt

Use The original edited-video glassmorphism look: crisp face-cam video, restrained dark frosted panels, white editorial text, rounded card edges, visible border highlights, and a heavy soft shadow. The motion should feel like a left-side information panel entering over a camera reframe, not like a full-screen takeover.

## Colors

- Canvas black: `#000000`
- Glass base: `rgba(8, 14, 26, 0.62)`
- Glass dim: `rgba(10, 18, 32, 0.58)`
- Border highlight: `rgba(125, 211, 252, 0.18)`
- Text: `#ffffff`
- Accent: `#7dd3fc`

## Typography

- Primary: `sans-serif`
- Label: `monospace`

## What Not To Do

- Do not cover the face in the center of the source frame.
- Do not use a full-screen dark overlay.
- Do not use decorative gradients behind the video.
- Do not truncate the source video for this prototype.
