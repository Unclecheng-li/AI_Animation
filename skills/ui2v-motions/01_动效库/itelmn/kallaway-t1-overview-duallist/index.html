What Actually Moves the Needle
Do This
Post on a consistent weekly schedule your audience can rely on.
Study your top 20% of videos — double down on what already works.
Open every video with the viewer's problem, not your introduction.
Build an email list from day one — you own that audience.
Avoid This
Chase trending topics that have nothing to do with your niche.
Spend 80% of your time on production, 20% on strategy — flip it.
Wait until you're "ready" — imperfect and shipped beats perfect and delayed.
Ignore analytics — data tells you what your gut can't.
