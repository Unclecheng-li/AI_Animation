# CutSteward trust demo — four-pass script development

Target: 15 seconds · 1080×1920 · English on-screen copy · international
creator/studio audience · original project-authored music · simple premium delivery.

## Pass 1 — Complete idea

AI video automation can move quickly, but it should never leave you guessing. CutSteward turns a
brief into a visible production run, pauses sensitive actions for approval, and keeps the evidence.
Bring your agent and build with confidence.

What works: full story and accurate product truth. What changes: too explanatory for 15 seconds;
the product name lands late; “build with confidence” is generic.

## Pass 2 — Value before evidence

Move fast without going blind. One brief becomes visible production. Sensitive actions stop at an
exact decision, and the result retains proof. Bring your agent. Keep control.

What works: outcome-language hook, product by beat two, trust proof after value. What changes:
“live, visible production run” is redundant; the close needs a branded action.

## Pass 3 — Visual rhythm and emotional arc

`MOVE FAST.` → `WITHOUT GOING BLIND.` → `ONE BRIEF` → `VISIBLE PRODUCTION.` →
`EXACT SCOPE` → `YOUR DECISION` → `PROOF THAT STAYS.` → `BRING YOUR AGENT.` →
`KEEP CONTROL.` → `BUILD WITH CUTSTEWARD.`

Visual arc: grounded tension on `BLIND`; lift and clarity when `CUTSTEWARD` appears;
quiet authority through the exact-decision proof; warm resolution across the three CTA cards.
Typography and shot rhythm carry the emotion without speech.

## Pass 4 — Layout, readability, and claim audit

Locked visual copy:

1. `MOVE FAST.` / `WITHOUT GOING BLIND.`
2. `CUTSTEWARD` / `ONE BRIEF` / `PLAN · RUN · REVIEW` / `VISIBLE PRODUCTION.`
3. `SENSITIVE ACTION` / `EXACT SCOPE` / `YOUR DECISION` / `REQUEST SCOPE · USER DECISION · VERIFIED OUTPUT` / `PROOF THAT STAYS.`
4. `BRING YOUR AGENT.` / `KEEP CONTROL.` / `BUILD WITH CUTSTEWARD.` / `OPEN SOURCE · MIT`

Readability check: each phrase group stays above the portrait platform keep-out,
and the final lockup remains resolved for 3.25 seconds. Claim check: no fake metric,
provider execution, customer, review, or endorsement.

## Lock decision

Pass 4 is the production copy. The public master is music-led and contains no
narration or spoken dialogue by user direction.
