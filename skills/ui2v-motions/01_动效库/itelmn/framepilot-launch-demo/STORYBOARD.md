## Video direction

Treat CutSteward as a premium instrument, not a neon AI toy. Use black and warm
ivory as the field, antique gold as the only authored accent, and the captured UI
without recoloring. One finite entrance per beat, then stillness. The screenshots
remain sharp and legible inside thin glass frames with restrained parallax. Use
hard or motivated match cuts; no particle fields, endless float, glow loops,
generic orbit rings, bounce, or high-frequency effects. End on at least 1.5s of
fully resolved brand and CTA. The opening Pexels clip is visual context only and
must never imply that its depicted person uses or endorses CutSteward.
