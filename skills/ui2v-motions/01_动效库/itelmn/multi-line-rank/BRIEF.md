## Intent

Demonstrate the reusable multi-line ranking template with generic data. Every path, endpoint, live value, legend position, date, and final ranking is derived from the HyperFrames timeline's seek position.

## Notes

- This is a HyperFrames template project, not an independent video runtime.
- The dataset is generated locally before preview or render.
- Rank motion must glide across crossovers and remain correct under backward seeking.
- Keep the bundled preview quick; generated compositions obtain pacing from `--pace` or an exact duration.
- Use the bundled upbeat electronic bed at a restrained level, with timeline-driven fades matching the opening and closing shutters.
- Open with a split-shutter reveal, stage the chart and ranking panel into place, then stagger the final ranking before the matching shutter closes.
