# Hero Ticker Tape

A serif breaking headline sits above a continuous amber ticker. Functionally it is a live-feed metaphor for registry activity.

## Purpose
- Newsroom and launch-day energy
- Continuous horizontal scroll patterns
- Dark canvas with high-contrast tape

## Specs
- Tape translates on x over 3.4 seconds
- Duplicate copy keeps the loop seamless in-frame

## Usage
Install and preview. Avoid slowing the tape or words become unreadable.

## Notes
Uppercase tracking is intentional; do not mix sentence case on the tape.
