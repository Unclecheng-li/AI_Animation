# STORYBOARD — wind-comic-promo

The whole video argues one thing: **every tool can generate a clip; this one lets you direct it.**
Frames 03–05 are the mechanism (the two capabilities that make direction possible), 06–07 are the
scope and the proof, 08 is the ask. Delete 03–07 and frames 01/02/08 still state the claim —
that is the value-before-evidence check from `story-spine.md` § 2.

## Video direction

- **Register.** Museum-catalogue restraint, not a SaaS sizzle reel: warm parchment ground, near-black
  ink, brand gold as the only accent. Every frame carries one sun-bloom for depth — no shadows, no
  rounded corners, 1px hairlines as the only rule. The product is precise; the film should be too.
- **Typography does the work.** Display sizes carry the claims; JetBrains Mono carries every number,
  path and directive string. Nothing is stated in colour that isn't stated in type.
- **Pacing.** Each frame develops across its whole duration — reveal paced to the narration, never
  front-loaded then frozen. Frames 03–05 are the argument's core and get the longest holds.
- **The gold is rationed.** It marks exactly one thing per frame: the take counter (01), the verb
  "direct" (02), the directive string (03), the audit lines (04), the two retaken seconds (05), the
  active stage (06), the two named shots (07), the repo line (08).
- **Honesty constraints.** Every number on screen is real product output (4131 tests; shot scores 52
  and 60 in frame 07). No engine-specific aspect-ratio claims — HappyHorse 9:16 is not live-verified.
- **Transitions.** Cuts inside an argument, crossfades between arguments (02, 05, 08).
