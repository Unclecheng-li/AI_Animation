## Intent

Create a smooth, friendly, awesome, motion-heavy launch video for the TDrive
desktop app. Market the product through a clear transformation: Telegram-backed
storage becomes a polished private drive for organizing, previewing, streaming,
protecting, sharing, mounting, and automating files.

## Customizations

- Use a 105-second modular cut that can later be shortened to 90 seconds.
- Keep headlines as live text and the expressive cloud, file-card, path, status,
  and cursor elements as independently editable layers.
- Use real desktop UI and source-supported claims; animated illustrations are
  supporting metaphors, not replacements for the product.
- Text-led storytelling with an original temporary music bed and restrained UI SFX.
