# Hero Vinyl Spin

A dark vinyl disc spins while a tonearm drops onto the groove. Function: **audio object / playback start** — music and media product energy without waveforms.

## Purpose

- Music, podcast, and media heroes
- Physical object choreography
- Warm stone palette with a rose label

## Specs

- Disc rotates continuously after entrance
- Tonearm rotates from raised to engaged
- Entry: `index.html`

## Usage

```bash
npx hyperframes preview
ui2v motion publish . --version 1.0.0
```

## Notes

Keep the center label short (UI2V). Long words break the circular lockup.
