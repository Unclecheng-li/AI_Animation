## Video direction

- Palette system: `frame.md` is normative. Near-black `#050505` is the canvas; warm ivory `#F4F0E8` is primary type; antique gold `#CDBA91` is the single scarce accent; `#8B7A58` and `#5C584F` are secondary rules and quiet labels. Preserve all UI screenshots in their native color.
- Motion grammar: smooth long-tail settles (`power3` register), hard editorial seams, and music-led sequential reveals. At t=0, show only the first visual idea; develop across the back half of each frame. Use one motivated camera move per frame at most.
- Rhythm: Frame 1 is a 0.90-second human microshot followed by two decisive type beats. Frames 2 and 3 let the UI do the proving. Frame 4 is the allocated breather: its final lockup resolves at 11.75s global time and remains readable for 3.25 seconds.
- Holds: Frame 2 holds the visible-production surface; Frame 3 holds exact approval and receipt language; Frame 4's final card is fully static. No lazy breathing or late camera push; subtle deterministic jitter is allowed only if a held element otherwise appears frozen.
- Captions: keep all meaningful product UI, headlines, and proof labels above the bottom ~17% of the portrait canvas. Limit caption groups to short English phrases and never cover an approval or receipt detail.
- Negative list: no bounce, elastic overshoot, particles, confetti, orbiting chrome, purple-blue AI gradients, glass overload, fake cursors, generic device stock mockups, fake provider receipts, fake metrics, floating screensaver motion, slideshow front-loading, or filters over real UI.
